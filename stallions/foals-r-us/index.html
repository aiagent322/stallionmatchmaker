Shining Spark

Offspring $10.4M+ · NRHA Hall of Fame · NRCHA Hall of Fame · 1994 NRHA Derby Champion · #1 All-Time Maternal Grandsire of Cow Horses
$5,000

Shining Spark was a 1989 palomino son of Genuine Doc out of AQHA Superhorse and Hall of Fame member Diamonds Sparkle, owned by AQHA Hall of Fame horsewoman Carol Rose. The 1994 NRHA Derby Champion, NRHA Hall of Fame inductee (2011), and NRCHA Hall of Fame inductee (2022), he became the youngest NRHA Million Dollar Sire in 2002 and ultimately crossed $10.4M+ in total offspring earnings. He is the #1 all-time maternal grandsire of reined cow horses with broodmare produce earnings exceeding $27M. Shining Spark passed away December 2021. Available via ICSI only through Foals-R-Us at $5,000 — an elite opportunity to access one of the most influential sires in Western performance horse history.
