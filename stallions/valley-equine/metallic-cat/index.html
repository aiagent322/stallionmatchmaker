About Metallic Cat

Metallic Cat is a proven champion and one of the most dominant sires the industry has ever seen. He led the NCHA sire standings every year from 2016 through 2024, and he has also held the NRCHA #1 leading sire position from 2018 through 2024. In 2024, he was the QData #1 Leading Cutting Sire and the #1 NCHA and NRCHA Leading Sire. In 2025, he is the QData #1 Reined Cow Horse Sire. He is also the QData and NCHA #2 All-Time Leading Sire and the QData and NRCHA #1 All-Time Leading Sire, with a record that includes siring the 2019 NCHA Open Futurity Champion.
